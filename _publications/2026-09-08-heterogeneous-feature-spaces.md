**Public preprint.** Zejing Zheng, Rui Huang, and Junlong Zhao. *Transfer Learning with Heterogeneous Feature Spaces in Linear Regression*. arXiv:2609.08526, 2026. Submitted 8 September 2026. [Public preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08526).

The paper studies transfer learning in low- and high-dimensional linear regression when source studies observe subsets of the target variables. It examines how differences in feature coverage and usable source information affect estimation accuracy. Improvements over target-only estimation are established under stated conditions.
